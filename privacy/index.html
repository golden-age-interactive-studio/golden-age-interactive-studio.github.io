Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoldenAgeInteractive handles information when you use the GoldenAgeInteractive Marketing Agent and its connected social media integrations.

1. Information We Access

When you choose to connect a supported social media account, the Marketing Agent may access information made available through the platform's official API and the permissions you approve.

This may include:

  • Basic account or profile information.
  • Account identifiers required to connect your account.
  • Publishing permissions that you explicitly authorize.
  • Information required to upload or publish marketing content on your behalf.
  • Status information related to uploaded or published content.

The Marketing Agent only requests permissions required for the features you choose to use.

2. TikTok Integration

If you connect a TikTok account, the Marketing Agent may use TikTok's official APIs to identify the connected account and, where authorized, upload marketing videos to TikTok.

TikTok content is uploaded only after an explicit action by the user. The Marketing Agent does not automatically publish TikTok content without user authorization.

3. Other Social Media Integrations

The Marketing Agent may also connect to services such as YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram through their official APIs.

Information accessed through these services is used only to provide requested features such as account connection, content preparation, uploading, and publishing.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate connected social media accounts.
  • Create and manage marketing publications.
  • Upload approved images and videos.
  • Track publication status.
  • Provide campaign and attribution information.
  • Maintain the security and operation of the service.

5. Authentication Credentials

OAuth access and refresh tokens used by connected services are stored securely by the Marketing Agent and are not intentionally exposed through the user interface.

Authentication credentials are used only to perform actions authorized by the account owner.

6. Data Sharing

GoldenAgeInteractive does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to the social media platforms you explicitly connect when necessary to provide publishing or account functionality.

7. Data Retention

Information required for connected accounts and publication history may be retained while the integration remains active.

You may disconnect a connected social media account at any time. Access credentials associated with that connection may then be removed or invalidated.

8. Temporary Media Access

When a social media platform requires access to an image or video through a public URL, the Marketing Agent may create a temporary, restricted link for that specific media file.

These links are intended only for transferring approved media to the selected platform and are not intended to provide general public access to local files.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose whether to connect a supported social media account.
  • Decline requested platform permissions.
  • Disconnect connected accounts.
  • Choose whether content is uploaded or published.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account credentials and application data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Services

Connected platforms such as TikTok, Google, Meta, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

GoldenAgeInteractive is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when functionality, integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the Marketing Agent, contact GoldenAgeInteractive through the contact information provided on the official GoldenAgeInteractive website.
